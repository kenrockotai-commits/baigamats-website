Who it's for

Set up for the way your business works

Each business gets its own space, its own staff and its own numbers. Choose the functions you need and switch on more as you grow.

Shops and boutiques

Point of sale, stock levels, prices per customer type, receipts and a daily cash close.

Restaurants and bars

Waiters take orders at the table, the kitchen and bar see them, and each guest pays how they like.

Tailors and fashion houses

Work orders, measurements, fittings, and a tracking link customers open on their phone.

Agrovets

Stock, sales and receipts for farm inputs, with every sale recorded the moment it is paid.

Wholesalers and distributors

Purchases, suppliers, stock movements and invoices in one tidy record.

What it does

The daily work, taken care of

Sell fastTap items, take payment, print or send the receipt.
Know your stockPurchases, suppliers and stock counts that match the shelf.
Control discountsOwners set how much staff may discount before approval.
Close the dayCash, Mobile Money and card totals counted against what the system expects.
Keep your booksSales, expenses, payables and receivables feeding accounts your accountant can use.
Run your teamStaff accounts with roles, so each person sees only what their job needs.

Payments

Get paid the way your customers pay

Take cash, Mobile Money and cards at the till. A sale is marked paid only when the payment provider confirms it, so a pending payment never passes as money received.

Mobile Money

Send a payment request to the customer's MTN or Airtel phone and watch it confirm on screen.

Cards

Accept cards through a hosted checkout, with the sale recorded automatically.

Bills and utilities

Pay suppliers and utilities from your business balance, with every payment logged.
