Who it's for

One platform, set up for the way your business works

Each business gets its own space, its own staff and its own numbers. Pick the functions you need and switch on more as you grow.

Retail

Shops and boutiques

Point of sale, stock levels, prices per customer type, receipts and daily cash close.

Hospitality

Restaurants and bars

Waiters take orders at the table, the kitchen and bar see them, and the bill is split how guests want to pay.

Tailoring

Tailors and fashion houses

Work orders, measurements, fittings and a tracking link customers can open on their phone.

Agrovet

Agro-input dealers

Stock, sales and customer credit for farm inputs, with receipts for every sale.

Finance

Savings and credit groups

Member records, repayment schedules and statements, kept in one place.

What it does

The daily work, taken care of

  • ◆
    Sell fastScan or tap items, take payment, print or send the receipt.
  • ◆
    Know your stockPurchases, suppliers and stock counts that match what is on the shelf.
  • ◆
    Control discountsOwners set how much staff may discount before approval is needed.
  • ◆
    Close the dayCash, Mobile Money and card totals counted against what the system expects.
  • ◆
    Keep your booksSales, expenses, payables and receivables flowing into accounts your accountant can use.
  • ◆
    Run your teamStaff accounts with roles, so each person sees only what their job needs.
  • ◆
    Talk to customersReceipts and updates by SMS and WhatsApp from your own number.
  • ◆
    Work from a phoneAn Android app for staff, with Bluetooth receipt printing.
Payments

Get paid the way your customers pay

Take cash, Mobile Money and cards at the till. A sale is only marked paid when the payment provider confirms it, so a pending payment never passes as money received.

Mobile Money

MTN and Airtel

Send a payment request to the customer's phone and watch it confirm on screen.

Cards

Card payments

Accept cards through a hosted checkout, with the sale recorded automatically.

Bills

Pay bills and utilities

Pay suppliers and utilities from your business balance, with every payment logged.
